# Helping more people use First Light

The purpose of the community edition is to make it easy to begin reading and exploring biblical art. Our first measure of success is whether a person can open a page, understand what to do, and find a reason to return.

## What is available now

- An English-first repository introduction and a complete Chinese introduction.
- Language-specific links to a painting, a passage, and a short lesson.
- A working website that visitors can use without an account or installation.
- Share links that preserve the current page and language.
- A public review form asking about usefulness, difficulty, and willingness to return.
- [Reusable descriptions in both languages](SHARE.md), with accurate scope and limitations.

## What the numbers can tell us

| Signal | What it supports | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub repository views and unique visitors | Interest in the repository over the available reporting window | App readers, completed lessons, or retention |
| Stars, forks, and watchers | Repository attention and developer interest | Number of people using the app |
| Voluntary experience reviews | Specific reports of usefulness or problems | A representative survey of every visitor |
| Website visits, if analytics is enabled and disclosed | Aggregate visits under the provider's definition | Individual people, reading completion, or long-term retention |

There is no ranking claim in our promotional copy. Any future position comparison must name the query or topic, sorting method, date, and comparison set. Search ordering can change, and a topic position is not a GitHub-wide product ranking.

Our daily repository report keeps unavailable data separate from zero. We do not count automated checks as evidence of real adoption. Website statistics must be independently verified before we report them as active.

## How we improve

We start with one concrete invitation, such as viewing *The Finding of Moses*. We check that the linked page opens in the intended language and that its next step works. We use real feedback to address confusing instructions, inaccessible controls, broken links, and device-specific speech problems.

We do not buy stars, submit fake reviews, or post the same advertisement into unrelated projects. Contributions should help the audience of the receiving community and follow its rules.
